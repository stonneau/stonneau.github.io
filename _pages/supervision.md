## Work with me

My research is on contact-rich motion planning and control for legged robots and humanoids: contact planning, multi-contact locomotion, model predictive control and whole-body manipulation, with a link to computer graphics and character animation. The [projects](/projects/) and [publications](/publications/) pages show what this looks like in practice.

The way to work with me as a PhD student is the **UKRI AI Centre for Doctoral Training in Dependable and Deployable AI for Robotics** ([CDT-D2AIR](https://www.cdt-d2air.uk/)). I am its deputy director.

### The CDT-D2AIR in short

- A four-year PhD programme offered jointly by Heriot-Watt University and the University of Edinburgh, part of the Edinburgh Centre for Robotics. It combines training in robotics, machine learning, optimisation, software engineering, verification and testing with a research project.
- Four research themes: trustworthy and rigorous AI for robotics, robust robot design, dependable AI for human-robot interaction, and AI for deployable robot systems and field systems.
- Studentships are fully funded: tuition fees, a monthly stipend at the UKRI rate, and a budget for travel, small equipment and an optional international placement. A small number of studentships for international applicants are available each year.
- In-person and full-time, based near Edinburgh.

### How to apply (September 2027 intake)

- **Deadline: Friday 27 November 2026**, for the first round of places. First-round decisions are aimed for January 2027, and offers for March 2027.
- Entry requirement: an undergraduate degree at UK 2:1 level (or equivalent) in computer science, engineering or a related discipline, with strong programming and quantitative skills. Applicants from other disciplines are considered with substantial exposure to computing or engineering. English-language requirements apply.
- Apply through the Heriot-Watt postgraduate application website, selecting _Dependable and Deployable Artificial Intelligence for Robotics, PhD_. A supplementary information form replaces the research proposal.
- **List me as a potential supervisor** in the supplementary form if my work interests you. You do not need to contact supervisors before applying: students define their PhD project during the first year, with help to choose a supervisory team.

Everything is detailed, and kept up to date, on the CDT's [apply page](https://www.cdt-d2air.uk/apply) and [FAQ](https://www.cdt-d2air.uk/faq). For questions about the programme itself, use the CDT's [contact page](https://www.cdt-d2air.uk/contact). For anything else, see my [contact](/contact/) page.
