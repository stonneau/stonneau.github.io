I am overwhelmed with PhD applications.

Most of you will have seen that the way to apply is to go through the [CDT-D2AIR](https://www.cdt-d2air.uk/).

The vast majority of the e-mails I receive show that no significant effort has been made to read and understand my research. Given the number of e-mails I receive every day, I have decided not to answer any application that does not reflect a sincere interest in, and understanding of, our approach.

This does not mean that you should not apply to the CDT, by all means, or that we will not end up working together eventually.
