The UKRI AI Centre for Doctoral Training in Dependable and Deployable AI for Robotics ([CDT-D2AIR](https://www.cdt-d2air.uk/)), run jointly by Heriot-Watt University and the University of Edinburgh, is recruiting. I am its deputy director. The four-year, fully funded PhD programme starts in September 2027 and applications are open: the deadline for the first round is **Friday 27 November 2026**.

Details, eligibility and the application procedure are on the [apply page](https://www.cdt-d2air.uk/apply).
