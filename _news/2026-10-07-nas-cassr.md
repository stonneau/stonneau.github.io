A new [project page](/projects/nas-cassr/) presents our two recent footstep planners for humanoids, led by Jiayi Wang: **NAS** (Humanoids 2024), which computes all the solutions of the contact planning problem to obtain a globally optimal policy, and **CASSR** (ICRA 2026), an A\* search that propagates continuous reachability constraints and plans up to 30 footsteps in under 125 ms. Both come with a video.
